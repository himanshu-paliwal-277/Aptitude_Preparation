## Error Spotting

### 1. Identify the part with an error:

"She do not want to go to the party."

- A) She
- B) do not
- C) want to
- D) go to the party

**✅ Answer:** B) do not
**📘 Explanation:** 'She' is third person singular. Correct form: **does not**

---

### 2. Identify the error:

"Each of the students have submitted their homework."

- A) Each of the students
- B) have submitted
- C) their homework
- D) No error

**✅ Answer:** B) have submitted
**📘 Explanation:** 'Each' is singular, so it should be **has submitted**

---

### 3. Identify the error:

"He is senior than me in college."

- A) He is
- B) senior
- C) than me
- D) in college

**✅ Answer:** C) than me
**📘 Explanation:** Use "to" with senior. Correct sentence: **He is senior to me**
