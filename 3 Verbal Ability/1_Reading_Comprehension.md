
## Reading Comprehension

### 1. Passage:

**"Technology has significantly impacted the way we live and work. From communication to transportation, every aspect of our life has been transformed. While some argue that this change is for the better, others believe it has made us overly dependent on machines."**

**Question:** What is the main idea of the passage?

* A) Technology has only improved communication.
* B) Machines have made life harder.
* C) Technology has changed every aspect of life.
* D) People dislike machines.

**✅ Answer:** C) Technology has changed every aspect of life.

---

### 2. According to the passage, some people think:

* A) Technology is completely bad.
* B) Technology is a gift.
* C) Technology makes us dependent.
* D) Technology helps in agriculture only.

**✅ Answer:** C) Technology makes us dependent.

---

### 3. What is the tone of the passage?

* A) Critical
* B) Balanced
* C) Humorous
* D) Indifferent

**✅ Answer:** B) Balanced
