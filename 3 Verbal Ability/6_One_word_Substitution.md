## One Word Substitution

### 1. A person who eats too much

* A) Glutton
* B) Miser
* C) Cynic
* D) Stoic

**✅ Answer:** A) Glutton

---

### 2. A person who knows everything

* A) Omnipresent
* B) Omnipotent
* C) Omniscient
* D) Omnivore

**✅ Answer:** C) Omniscient

---

### 3. A speech delivered without preparation

* A) Extempore
* B) Dialogue
* C) Debate
* D) Commentary

**✅ Answer:** A) Extempore
