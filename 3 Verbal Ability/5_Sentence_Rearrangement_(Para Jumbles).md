## Sentence Rearrangement

### 1. Rearrange to form a meaningful sentence:

**P: always P: to school S: goes Rohan Q: on time**

- A) P Q R S
- B) Rohan goes to school always on time
- C) S P Q R
- D) S Q R P

**✅ Answer:** B) Rohan goes to school always on time

---

### 2. Arrange the sentence:

**P: live in the desert. Q: Camels R: with little water S: and can survive**

- A) Q P S R
- B) Q P R S
- C) Q S P R
- D) Q R P S

**✅ Answer:** B) Q P R S

---
