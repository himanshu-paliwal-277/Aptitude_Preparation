## Synonyms & Antonyms

### 1. Choose the synonym of the word **"Abandon"**

- A) Keep
- B) Leave
- C) Hold
- D) Guard

**✅ Answer:** B) Leave
**📘 Explanation:**
"Abandon" means to leave something completely.

---

### 2. Choose the antonym of the word **"Optimistic"**

- A) Hopeful
- B) Cheerful
- C) Pessimistic
- D) Positive

**✅ Answer:** C) Pessimistic
**📘 Explanation:**
"Optimistic" means hopeful or positive, while "pessimistic" is the opposite.

---

### 3. Choose the synonym of **"Brief"**

- A) Long
- B) Short
- C) Deep
- D) Wide

**✅ Answer:** B) Short
**📘 Explanation:**
"Brief" refers to something short in duration or length.

---

### 4. Choose the antonym of **"Hostile"**

- A) Angry
- B) Aggressive
- C) Friendly
- D) Cold

**✅ Answer:** C) Friendly
**📘 Explanation:**
"Hostile" means unfriendly or antagonistic, while "friendly" is its antonym.

---

### 5. Choose the synonym of **"Enormous"**

- A) Tiny
- B) Huge
- C) Light
- D) Flat

**✅ Answer:** B) Huge
**📘 Explanation:**
"Enormous" means very large or massive.
