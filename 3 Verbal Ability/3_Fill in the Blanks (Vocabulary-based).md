## Fill in the Blanks / Synonyms & Antonyms

### 1. She is known for her \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_ nature and never hurts anyone.

- A) arrogant
- B) humble
- C) rude
- D) strict

**✅ Answer:** B) humble

---

### 2. SYNONYM of “Benevolent”

- A) Cruel
- B) Generous
- C) Harsh
- D) Selfish

**✅ Answer:** B) Generous

---

### 3. ANTONYM of “Obscure”

- A) Clear
- B) Unknown
- C) Faded
- D) Dim

**✅ Answer:** A) Clear
