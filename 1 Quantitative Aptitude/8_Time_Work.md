## ⏱ Time and Work

### 1. A can complete a work in 20 days, B in 30 days. How long will they take together?

- A) 10 days
- B) 12 days
- C) 15 days
- D) 25 days

**✅ Answer:** B) 12 days
**📘 Explanation:**
A's 1 day work = 1/20, B's = 1/30
Together = 1/20 + 1/30 = (3 + 2)/60 = 5/60 = 1/12
→ Time = 12 days

---

### 2. A and B can complete a piece of work in 8 days. B alone can complete it in 12 days. How long will A take alone?

- A) 18 days
- B) 20 days
- C) 24 days
- D) 30 days

**✅ Answer:** C) 24 days
**📘 Explanation:**
A + B = 1/8, B = 1/12 → A = 1/8 - 1/12 = (3 – 2)/24 = 1/24
→ A takes 24 days

---

### 3. If 10 men can do a job in 12 days, how many men are needed to do it in 8 days?

- A) 12
- B) 15
- C) 16
- D) 18

**✅ Answer:** C) 15
**📘 Explanation:**
Men × Days = Constant
10 × 12 = x × 8 → x = 120/8 = 15
