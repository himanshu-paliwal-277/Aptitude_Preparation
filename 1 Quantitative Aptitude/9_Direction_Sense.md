## 🚦 Distance & Relative Speed

### 1. A car travels 180 km in 3 hours. What is its average speed?

- A) 50 km/h
- B) 60 km/h
- C) 70 km/h
- D) 80 km/h

**✅ Answer:** B) 60 km/h
**📘 Explanation:**
Speed = Distance / Time = 180 / 3 = 60 km/h

---

### 2. Two trains running in opposite directions cross each other in 10 seconds. Their speeds are 36 km/h and 54 km/h. Find the length of the faster train if the other is 180 m long.

- A) 100 m
- B) 150 m
- C) 180 m
- D) 270 m

**✅ Answer:** D) 270 m
**📘 Explanation:**
Relative speed = 36 + 54 = 90 km/h = 25 m/s
Distance = Speed × Time = 25 × 10 = 250 m
Length of faster train = 250 – 180 = 70 m

---

### 3. A man walks at 5 km/h. He is 600 meters ahead of a boy who runs at 8 km/h. How long will it take the boy to catch the man?

- A) 5 min
- B) 10 min
- C) 12 min
- D) 15 min

**✅ Answer:** B) 12 min
**📘 Explanation:**
Relative speed = 8 – 5 = 3 km/h = 3000 m/h
Time = Distance / Speed = 600 / 3000 = 1/5 hour = 12 min
