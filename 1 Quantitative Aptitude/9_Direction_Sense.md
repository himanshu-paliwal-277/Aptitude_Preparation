## 🚦 Distance & Relative Speed

### 1. A car travels 180 km in 3 hours. What is its average speed?

- A) 50 km/h
- B) 60 km/h
- C) 70 km/h
- D) 80 km/h

**✅ Answer:** B) 60 km/h
**📘 Explanation:**
Speed = Distance / Time = 180 / 3 = 60 km/h

---

### 2. Two trains running in opposite directions cross each other in 10 seconds. Their speeds are 36 km/h and 54 km/h. Find the length of the faster train if the other is 180 m long.

- A) 80 m
- B) 250 m
- C) 100 m
- D) 70 m

**✅ Answer:** D) 70 m
**📘 Explanation:**
Relative speed = 36 + 54 = 90 km/h = 25 m/s
Distance = Speed × Time = 25 × 10 = 250 m
Length of faster train = 250 – 180 = 70 m

---

### 3. A man walks at 5 km/h. He is 600 meters ahead of a boy who runs at 8 km/h. How long will it take the boy to catch the man?

- A) 5 min
- B) 10 min
- C) 12 min
- D) 15 min

**✅ Answer:** C) 12 min
**📘 Explanation:**
Relative speed = 8 – 5 = 3 km/h = 3000 m/h
Time = Distance / Speed = 600 / 3000 = 1/5 hour = 12 min

### 4. A man walks to his office at 4 km/h and returns at 6 km/h. What is his average speed for the whole journey?

- A) 5 km/h
- B) 4.8 km/h
- C) 5.2 km/h
- D) 4 km/h

**✅ Answer:** B) 4.8 km/h
**📘 Explanation:**
This is a **same distance** problem (going and coming back same distance).
Use the average speed formula for same distance:

$$
\text{Average Speed} = \frac{2xy}{x + y}
$$

Where $x = 4 \, \text{km/h}, \, y = 6 \, \text{km/h}$

$$
= \frac{2 \times 4 \times 6}{4 + 6} = \frac{48}{10} = \boxed{4.8 \, \text{km/h}}
$$

### 5. A train travels at 60 km/h for the first half of the time and at 90 km/h for the second half. What is the average speed for the whole journey?

- A) 72 km/h
- B) 75 km/h
- C) 78 km/h
- D) 80 km/h

**✅ Answer:** B) 75 km/h
**📘 Explanation:**
This is a **same time** problem. When time is same, average speed is the simple average:

$$
\text{Average Speed} = \frac{x + y}{2}
$$

Where $x = 60 \, \text{km/h}, \, y = 90 \, \text{km/h}$

$$
= \frac{60 + 90}{2} = \frac{150}{2} = \boxed{75 \, \text{km/h}}
$$

---

### 6. A car covers two distances of 120 km and 180 km at the same speed. What is the total time taken if the speed is 60 km/h?

- A) 5 hours
- B) 6 hours
- C) 4.5 hours
- D) 3 hours

**✅ Answer:** A) 5 hours
**📘 Explanation:**
This is a **same speed** problem. Time for each trip = Distance / Speed.

$$
\text{Time}_1 = \frac{120}{60} = 2 \, \text{hours}, \quad \text{Time}_2 = \frac{180}{60} = 3 \, \text{hours}
$$

$$
\text{Total Time} = 2 + 3 = \boxed{5 \, \text{hours}}
$$

### 7. A person travels 120 km by car at 60 km/h and returns the same distance by bike at 40 km/h. What is his average speed for the whole journey?

- A) 48 km/h
- B) 50 km/h
- C) 52 km/h
- D) 45 km/h

**✅ Answer:** A) 48 km/h
**📘 Explanation:**
This is an **average speed** problem with same distance both ways.

Use the formula for average speed when distances are equal:

$$
\text{Average Speed} = \frac{2xy}{x + y}
$$

Where $x = 60 \, \text{km/h}, \, y = 40 \, \text{km/h}$

$$
= \frac{2 \times 60 \times 40}{60 + 40} = \frac{4800}{100} = \boxed{48 \, \text{km/h}}
$$
