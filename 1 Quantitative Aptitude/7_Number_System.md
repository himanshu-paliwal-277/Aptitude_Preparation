## 📘 Number System

### 1. What is the smallest number that is divisible by 12, 15, and 20?

- A) 60
- B) 120
- C) 240
- D) 180

**✅ Answer:** A) 60
**📘 Explanation:**
LCM of 12, 15, and 20 = 60 → 2² × 3 × 5 = 60

---

### 2. A number when divided by 5 leaves a remainder 3. What will be the remainder when the square of the number is divided by 5?

- A) 1
- B) 2
- C) 3
- D) 4

**✅ Answer:** D) 4
**📘 Explanation:**
Let the number be x = 5k + 3
x² = (5k + 3)² = 25k² + 30k + 9 → remainder = 9 % 5 = 4

---

### 3. Which of the following is a prime number?

- A) 49
- B) 39
- C) 37
- D) 51

**✅ Answer:** C) 37
**📘 Explanation:**
37 has no divisors other than 1 and itself → Prime number.
