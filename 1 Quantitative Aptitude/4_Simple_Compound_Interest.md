# 💰 Simple & Compound Interest

### 1. What is the simple interest on ₹5000 for 3 years at 6% per annum?

* A) ₹600
* B) ₹800
* C) ₹900
* D) ₹1000

**✅ Answer:** C) ₹900
**📘 Explanation:**
SI = (P × R × T)/100 = (5000×6×3)/100 = ₹900

---

### 2. Compound interest on ₹4000 for 2 years at 5% per annum (compounded yearly)?

* A) ₹400
* B) ₹410
* C) ₹420
* D) ₹405

**✅ Answer:** B) ₹410
**📘 Explanation:**
CI = P(1 + R/100)^T – P
\= 4000(1.05)^2 – 4000 = 4000(1.1025 – 1) = ₹410

---

### 3. In how many years will ₹8000 amount to ₹9680 at 10% simple interest?

* A) 1.5 years
* B) 2 years
* C) 3 years
* D) 4 years

**✅ Answer:** B) 2 years
**📘 Explanation:**
SI = 9680 – 8000 = ₹1680
SI = (P×R×T)/100 → 1680 = (8000×10×T)/100
T = 1680 × 100 / (8000 × 10) = 2.1 ≈ 2
