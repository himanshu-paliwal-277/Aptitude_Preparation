# ⚖️ Ratio and Proportion

### 1. Divide ₹1200 between A and B in the ratio 2:3.

- A) ₹400 and ₹800
- B) ₹500 and ₹700
- C) ₹480 and ₹720
- D) ₹600 and ₹600

**✅ Answer:** C) ₹480 and ₹720
**📘 Explanation:**
Total parts = 2 + 3 = 5
A = (2/5)×1200 = ₹480, B = ₹720

### 2. If 3A = 4B, what is the ratio A\:B?

- A) 3:4
- B) 4:3
- C) 3:5
- D) 5:3

**✅ Answer:** B) 4:3
**📘 Explanation:**
A/B = 4/3 → A\:B = 4:3

### 3. A recipe requires ingredients in the ratio 2:3:5. If total weight is 100g, what is the weight of each?

- A) 20g, 30g, 50g
- B) 25g, 25g, 50g
- C) 10g, 30g, 60g
- D) 15g, 35g, 50g

**✅ Answer:** A) 20g, 30g, 50g
**📘 Explanation:**
Total parts = 2+3+5 = 10 → (2/10)×100 = 20g, (3/10)×100 = 30g, (5/10)×100 = 50g
