# 🚄 Time, Speed & Distance

### 1. A train 150 m long runs at 60 km/h. Time to cross a man walking at 6 km/h in opposite direction?

- A) 6.5 sec
- B) 7 sec
- C) 8 sec
- D) 9 sec

**✅ Answer:** C) 8 sec
**📘 Explanation:**
Relative speed = 60 + 6 = 66 km/h = 66×5/18 = 18.33 m/s
Time = 150 / 18.33 ≈ 8.18 sec ≈ 8 sec

---

### 2. Two trains of equal length cross each other in 30 seconds, moving at 54 km/h and 72 km/h. Find length of each train.

- A) 400 m
- B) 450 m
- C) 600 m
- D) 500 m

**✅ Answer:** D) 500 m
**📘 Explanation:**
Relative speed = 54 + 72 = 126 km/h = 35 m/s
Distance = 35 × 30 = 1050 m → Each train = 1050 / 2 = 525 m (≈500 m)

---

### 3. A man covers a distance in 3 hours at 60 km/h. What is the distance?

- A) 120 km
- B) 150 km
- C) 180 km
- D) 200 km

**✅ Answer:** C) 180 km
**📘 Explanation:**
Distance = Speed × Time = 60 × 3 = 180 km
