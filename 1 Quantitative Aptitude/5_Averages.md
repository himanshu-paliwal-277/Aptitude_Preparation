# 📊 Average

### 1. Find the average of first 10 natural numbers.

* A) 5
* B) 5.5
* C) 6
* D) 4.5

**✅ Answer:** B) 5.5
**📘 Explanation:**
Average = (1+2+3+...+10)/10 = 55/10 = 5.5

---

### 2. The average age of 5 people is 30 years. If one more joins and the average becomes 32, what is the age of the new person?

* A) 35
* B) 40
* C) 42
* D) 50

**✅ Answer:** C) 42
**📘 Explanation:**
Total age of 5 people = 30×5 = 150
New total = 32×6 = 192 → New person’s age = 192 – 150 = 42

---

### 3. Average score in 10 matches is 45. If 11th score is 60, what is the new average?

* A) 46.5
* B) 47
* C) 48
* D) 49

**✅ Answer:** A) 46.5
**📘 Explanation:**
Total = 45×10 = 450 → New total = 450 + 60 = 510
Average = 510 / 11 = 46.36 ≈ 46.5
