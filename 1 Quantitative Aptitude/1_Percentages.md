# Percentages

### 1. What is 20% of 150?

- A) 25
- B) 30
- C) 35
- D) 45

**✅ Answer:** B) 30  
**📘 Explanation:**  
20% of 150 = (20/100) × 150 = 30

---

### 2. A number is increased by 25% and then decreased by 20%. What is the net percentage change?

- A) 0%
- B) 5% increase
- C) 10% decrease
- D) 0.5% increase

**✅ Answer:** A) 0%  
**📘 Explanation:**  
Use successive change formula:  
Net % change = A + B + (A×B)/100  
= 25 – 20 + (25 × –20)/100  
= 5 – 5 = 0%

Net effect = **No change**

---

### 3. The price of a commodity is first increased by 30% and then decreased by 30%. What is the net percentage change in the price?

- A) 0%
- B) 9% increase
- C) 9% decrease
- D) 18% decrease

**✅ Answer:** C) 9% decrease  
**📘 Explanation:**  
Use the successive percentage change formula:  
Net % change = A + B + (A×B)/100  
= 30 – 30 + (30 × –30)/100 = 0 – 9 = –9%

So, the price decreases by **9%**
