## ✅ Profit and Loss

### 1. If a product is bought for ₹200 and sold for ₹250, what is the profit percentage?

* A) 20%
* B) 25%
* C) 30%
* D) 10%

**✅ Answer:** B) 25%
**📘 Explanation:** Profit = 250 - 200 = 50; (Profit/Cost Price) × 100 = (50/200)×100 = 25%

---

### 2. If a product is sold for ₹160 after a 20% loss, what was the cost price?

* A) ₹180
* B) ₹190
* C) ₹200
* D) ₹210

**✅ Answer:** C) ₹200
**📘 Explanation:** SP = 80% of CP ⇒ 160 = 0.8 × CP ⇒ CP = 160/0.8 = ₹200

---

### 3. A man buys an article for ₹500 and sells it at a loss of 10%. Find the selling price.

* A) ₹450
* B) ₹475
* C) ₹500
* D) ₹550

**✅ Answer:** A) ₹450
**📘 Explanation:** Loss = 10% of 500 = ₹50; SP = CP – Loss = 500 – 50 = ₹450
