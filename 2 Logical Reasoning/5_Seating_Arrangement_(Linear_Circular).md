## Seating Arrangement & Puzzles

### 1. A, B, C, D, and E are sitting in a row. A is to the right of B but left of C. D is to the right of C but left of E. Who is sitting in the middle?

* A) C
* B) D
* C) A
* D) B

**✅ Answer:** A) C
**📘 Explanation:** Order: B, A, C, D, E

---

### 2. Five people A, B, C, D, E are sitting around a circle. B is to the right of A, C is opposite B, D is between A and C. Who is to the left of D?

* A) E
* B) A
* C) C
* D) B

**✅ Answer:** C) C
**📘 Explanation:** Draw circle and position everyone step-by-step.

---

### 3. Four friends are sitting on a bench. A is to the left of B, but right of C. D is at one end. Who is sitting second from the left?

* A) C
* B) A
* C) B
* D) D

**✅ Answer:** B) A
**📘 Explanation:** Order: D, C, A, B
