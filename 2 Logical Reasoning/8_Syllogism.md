## Syllogism

### 1. Statements:

All dogs are animals. Some animals are cats.
Conclusion:
I. Some dogs are cats.
II. All dogs are cats.

- A) Only I follows
- B) Only II follows
- C) Both I and II follow
- D) Neither follows

**✅ Answer:** D) Neither follows

---

### 2. Statements:

All pens are books. Some books are papers.
Conclusion:
I. Some pens are papers.
II. All books are pens.

- A) Only I follows
- B) Only II follows
- C) Neither follows
- D) Both follow

**✅ Answer:** C) Neither follows

---

### 3. Statements:

Some apples are oranges. All oranges are bananas.
Conclusion:
I. Some apples are bananas.
II. All bananas are apples.

- A) Only I follows
- B) Only II follows
- C) Both follow
- D) Neither follows

**✅ Answer:** A) Only I follows
