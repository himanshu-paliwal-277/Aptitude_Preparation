## Number and Alphabet Series

### 1. Find the missing term: 2, 6, 12, 20, ?, 42

- A) 25
- B) 30
- C) 32
- D) 35

**✅ Answer:** C) 30
**📘 Explanation:** Differences: 4, 6, 8, 10... pattern of +2

---

### 2. What comes next in the series? A, C, F, J, O, ?

- A) U
- B) V
- C) W
- D) X

**✅ Answer:** A) U
**📘 Explanation:** Gaps are +2, +3, +4, +5, so next gap is +6: O + 6 = U

---

### 3. What comes next in the series? 5, 11, 23, 47, ?

- A) 95
- B) 93
- C) 97
- D) 99

**✅ Answer:** A) 95
**📘 Explanation:** Pattern is ×2 +1: 5×2+1=11, 11×2+1=23, etc.
