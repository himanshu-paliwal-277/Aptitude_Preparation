## Puzzles

### 1. Five people – A, B, C, D, and E – are sitting in a row. A is to the right of B but to the left of C. D is to the right of C. Who is sitting at the center?

- A) A
- B) B
- C) C
- D) D

**✅ Answer:** A) A
**📘 Explanation:** Order is B, A, C, D, E → A is at the center.

---

### 2. There are five books labeled A, B, C, D, and E. B is placed below C, A is above E, but below D. Which book is at the top?

- A) A
- B) C
- C) D
- D) E

**✅ Answer:** C) D
**📘 Explanation:** Order from top to bottom is: D, A, E and C, B → D is at top.

---

### 3. In a family of six members A, B, C, D, E, and F: A and B are a married couple, D is the only son of C, E is the brother of A, F is the daughter of B. Who is the father of D?

- A) A
- B) B
- C) C
- D) D

**✅ Answer:** C) C
**📘 Explanation:** Based on clues, C is the father of D.
