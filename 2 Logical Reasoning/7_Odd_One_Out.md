## Odd One Out

### 1. Choose the odd one:

- A) Apple
- B) Banana
- C) Mango
- D) Carrot

**✅ Answer:** D) Carrot
**📘 Explanation:** All others are fruits; carrot is a vegetable.

---

### 2. Choose the odd one:

- A) 2
- B) 3
- C) 5
- D) 9

**✅ Answer:** D) 9
**📘 Explanation:** 9 is not a prime number, others are.

---

### 3. Choose the odd one:

- A) Square
- B) Circle
- C) Rectangle
- D) Triangle

**✅ Answer:** B) Circle
**📘 Explanation:** All others have straight sides.
