## Direction Sense

### 1. A person walks 10 m north, then turns right and walks 10 m, then turns right and walks 10 m. In which direction is he from the starting point?

- A) North
- B) South
- C) East
- D) West

**✅ Answer:** C) East
**📘 Explanation:** Final position is 10 m east from starting point.

---

### 2. Ramesh walks 30 m towards North, then turns left and walks 20 m, again turns left and walks 30 m. How far is he from the starting point?

- A) 10 m
- B) 20 m
- C) 30 m
- D) 40 m

**✅ Answer:** B) 20 m
**📘 Explanation:** He is 20 m to the left (west) of the starting point.

---

### 3. A man walks 4 km towards north, then turns right and walks 5 km, then again turns right and walks 4 km. How far is he from the starting point?

- A) 3 km
- B) 4 km
- C) 5 km
- D) 9 km

**✅ Answer:** C) 5 km
**📘 Explanation:** He is 5 km east from the starting point.
